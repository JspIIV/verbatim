Verbatim: what a public page said, word for word, at the moment it was read, and every time it changed. A screenshot can be faked and a page can be quietly edited. record(url, question) has a round of GenLayer validators read the page and keep the exact passage on the question. A model picks the passage, but the proof is a string match: every validator must find those same characters in its own copy of the page, checked in code, so a hostile page cannot put words on the record that it does not contain. recheck(id), open to anybody, confirms unchanged words with no model and adds a quiet edit as a new reading beside the old one. A failed fetch is never a removal, only 404 or 410 reads GONE, and NOT_SAID needs the whole page. said_at(id, ts) answers only for moments a reading covers. Other contracts gate on still_says(id). Built from the Agent Tank pitch on proving what a source said when it was checked.
